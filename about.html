Our story

From a focused importer to a leader in interventional cardiology.

Sapphire International was established in 2012 and has been actively engaged in the sales and marketing of high-quality medical devices and instruments ever since. Under seasoned leadership, the company has achieved significant milestones and emerged as one of the fastest-growing and most respected organisations in Pakistan’s medical industry — particularly in interventional cardiology.

What sets us apart is a customer-centric approach, concept-driven marketing and a dedicated team of professionally trained experts. We have introduced and built a strong market presence for several international brands, securing a solid share in a highly competitive landscape.

Our notable product lines include drug-coated stents, bare-metal stents and a comprehensive range of angiography products — each contributing to our reputation and sustained growth within the medical community.

What guides us

Mission, vision and values.

Mission

To deliver accessible and dependable healthcare solutions that enhance lives — without exception. We are committed to ethical, science-driven marketing, ongoing training and development of our sales teams, empowering healthcare professionals through Continuing Medical Education (CME), and ensuring the availability of high-quality healthcare products to serve society at large.

Vision

To become a regional leader in healthcare by providing high-quality, value-focused medical products, driven by innovation, expertise and unwavering ethical standards. We aim to foster trust, responsibility and mutual respect in every partnership we build.

Core values

We honour respect for individuals and partnerships, uphold integrity in all our actions, and base our operations on professionalism, dedication and strict compliance.

Quality policy

We are committed to total customer satisfaction by consistently providing superior-quality products and systems that meet all regulatory standards. Our promise includes timely delivery, competitive pricing and excellence at every stage. Through proactive, customer-centric management and a culture of continuous improvement, we aim not only to meet but to exceed our clients’ expectations — every time.
