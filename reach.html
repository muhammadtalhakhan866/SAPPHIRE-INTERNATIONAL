Market performance & sales revenue

A strong and consistent upward trajectory.

Gross sales increased from PKR 3.27 million in 2021 to PKR 16.21 million in 2022, followed by a sharp rise to PKR 67.06 million in 2023. Momentum continued in 2024 with PKR 129.74 million, peaking at PKR 214.45 million in 2025.
